About us

Practical strategy for businesses that have outgrown guesswork

We help owner-led companies decide what to do next — and prove it with their own numbers before a single pound is committed.

Start a conversation How we work

Northbridge Consulting works with established, owner-led businesses at the point where instinct stops being enough. You already have customers, revenue and a team. What you need is a clear view of what to do next, in what order, and what it will genuinely cost you to get there.

We start with the numbers you already have. Before recommending anything, we spend time inside your operation — reading your accounts, sitting with your people and tracing how work actually moves through the business rather than how the org chart says it should. That groundwork is unglamorous, and it is the reason our recommendations survive contact with reality.

From there we build a plan you can act on: a short list of decisions, each with an owner, a deadline and a number attached. No 90-slide decks, no jargon, no strategy that quietly depends on hiring six people you cannot afford. If a recommendation cannot be implemented by your current team within the next two quarters, we say so plainly and help you sequence it properly.

Founded in 2014, we have guided more than 180 engagements across manufacturing, professional services, logistics and healthcare. Most of our work now comes from clients we advised years ago, which is the only performance metric we really trust. We keep our engagements deliberately small so the people you meet are the people who do the work.
